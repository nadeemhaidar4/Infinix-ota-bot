How to play

Everyone receives a secret word. Villagers share one word and the Spy receives a related word.

Players describe their word in turn without saying the word itself. After the description round, alive players vote.

If a villager is eliminated, the same category and word pair continue into Round 2, Round 3 and so on. If the Spy is eliminated, civilians win.

If only two alive players remain, the Spy wins.

Voice: press Get Ready or Enable microphone once so the browser can grant microphone permission. Your mic goes live only during your own speaking turn.

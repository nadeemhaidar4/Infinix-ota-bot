How to play

Everyone gets a word. Villagers receive the common word and the Spy receives a related word.

Players describe their word without saying it. After everyone speaks, alive players vote for the suspected Spy.

If a villager is eliminated, another description round begins with the same words. If the Spy is eliminated, civilians win. If only two players remain, the Spy wins.

Voice: Tap the microphone once to allow microphone access. During your turn the mic becomes active; other players can hear you live.

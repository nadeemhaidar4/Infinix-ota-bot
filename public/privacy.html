Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. No Data Collection

QuickSave does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require registration or login of any kind.

2. How It Works

When you paste a URL, our server temporarily processes it to extract the media file. URLs and videos are not stored on our servers. All processing is done in real-time and immediately discarded.

3. Cookies

We use only functional cookies to remember your preferences (e.g., auto-paste setting). We do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

4. Advertising

We display advertisements via third-party ad networks (Monetag). These networks may use cookies to display relevant ads. We do not control their data practices. Please review their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Links

Our service processes links from Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter. We are not affiliated with these platforms. Please refer to their privacy policies for information about how they handle your data.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related concerns, contact us at: your@email.com
